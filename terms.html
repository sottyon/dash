Sot Dash SMS Terms

These terms apply to the optional Sot Dash daily task reminder text-message program. By checking the consent box and saving your mobile number in the Sot Dash dashboard, you agree to receive recurring automated SMS messages directly from Sot Dash at the number you provide.

Program details

The program sends up to one task reminder per day, generally around 8:00 a.m. in the time zone selected in your dashboard. Message frequency may vary. Messages may include task titles and categories saved in your dashboard.

Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing or using any product or service.

Opt out and help

To stop reminders, reply STOP to a message or turn off reminders in the Sot Dash dashboard. You will no longer receive daily reminders after your opt-out is processed. For help with dashboard reminder settings, use the reminder settings in your Sot Dash account.

Privacy

Your mobile number and SMS opt-in information are used to provide this reminder program. They are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process this information only to operate and deliver the messages for Sot Dash. See the Sot Dash Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about these terms or the reminder program, use the support channel through which you access your Sot Dash account.
