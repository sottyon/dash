Sot Dash daily task text reminders

Sot Dash can text you one reminder each morning (around 8:00 a.m. in your time zone) listing your open Work and Personal tasks. The program is optional and you can turn it off at any time.

Program details

  • Sender: Sot Dash
  • Messages: recurring automated daily task reminders, up to 1 message per day
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message, or pause reminders on this page
  • Help: reply HELP to any message
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase. Your mobile number and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
