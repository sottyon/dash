Sot Dash Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Sot Dash handles information for the optional daily task reminder SMS program.

Information and use

If you opt in, Sot Dash stores your mobile number, selected time zone, and consent status to provide and manage your reminders. Task titles and categories from your dashboard may be included in the reminders. Your number and consent information are used to operate the service, respond to your settings, and honor opt-outs.

Service providers and marketing

Sot Dash does not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. A messaging provider may process the information only on Sot Dash's behalf to deliver the texts. It may not use that information to market its own or another party's products or services.

Your choices

You may pause reminders in your dashboard or reply STOP to a reminder. Pausing the program prevents future reminder messages until you resume them on the text reminders page. Consent is recorded once for each mobile number; entering a different number requires agreeing again.

For the program's schedule, rates, and other messaging details, read the Sot Dash SMS Terms.
